“I'm building a life-like mini Totoro robot that sits on your desk to help you 
achieve productivity goals. Its ears and nose act as buttons to switch into different 
modes, whether you want to be in ‘focus mode’, ‘break mode’, or ‘normal’. The normal 
mode features life-like movements of the Totoro, including blinking. The different modes 
appear on its belly, replaced by a display screen, in order to indicate the current mode. 
The product aims to encourage and maintain blocks of focus and breaks to effectively make 
progress in one's life.”

Roadmap ->
- CAD design (+prototype mechanical concepts)
    - configuration of electronics
    - snap-fit mechanisms
    - Totoro shape
- 3D print parts
- Assemble + wire electronics
- Code functions
